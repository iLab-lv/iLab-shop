## Preserve / intentionally excluded

- Admin workflow and reusable editing components are assets. Do not restyle Admin purely to match public templates.
- Keep Brand -> Series -> Model hierarchy, independent Product slugs, derived compatibility, wholesale permission helpers, runtime settings and Service destination mapping.
- No evidence justifies Redux, search SaaS, new Firestore search fields, a new CMS or a complete rewrite.
- Online gateways, credit-note automation, ERP, reservation expiry, customer self-cancellation, carrier lookup and promotional codes are new scope or explicitly deferred; a reference mock does not approve them.
- Product quality badges, ratings, delivery promises, related-product algorithms and structured specifications require real data/requirements. Do not populate them from template fiction.
- Permanent Order/Invoice deletion is an explicit current business rule. Accounting retention review is a decision gate, not an instruction to change it based on assumed law.
