## Executive summary

The approved redesign contains five concrete patterns that are absent or only partially represented in Shop. Search exists and retains dynamic catalog logic, but its presentation is a dark full-screen panel with compact rounded result cards rather than the reference’s counter-style search with model and product columns. The reference rails provide working section and scroll navigation; Shop has no corresponding markup or controller. Product cards have some editorial overrides, but the effective Shop product grid remains four columns above 1279px, two columns at 1024px and below, and one column at 600px and below; the reference is three columns above 1023px and two columns through 1023px. Product detail preserves the correct data and purchase flow, yet still uses the older rounded gallery, compact title, pill/category styles and card-like compatibility panels. The reference implements a page-cover transition with GSAP; Shop only animates panels and controls, with no route transition.

## Reference-to-production mapping

### S3R.1 — Search

- **Reference:** `src/partials/header-shop.html` (search entry control); `src/partials/panels-shop.html` (full-width `aside.drawer--full.counter`, `.search-box`, `.search-layout`, `.search-models`, product result rows and inquiry); `assets/css/shop.css` (`.drawer--full`, `.search-box`, `.search-layout`, `.srow`); `assets/js/shop.js` search section around `run()` (models + product matches, clear, show-more, inquiry context and focus on open). The reference input is a large, transparent, underlined editorial field. At `min-width:1024px`, models occupy 3/12 and products 9/12; smaller screens stack them. Result rows use a 72px image and text/price/action columns with hairline separators.
- **Shop:** `ShopHeader.js` entry button; `SearchPanel.js`, `SearchContext.js`, `SearchPanel.module.css`, `ShopFullscreenPanel.js` and `.module.css`.
- **Discrepancy:** Search is functional, loads its in-memory catalog data and supports model suggestions, but the composition differs: rounded shadowed input, floating autocomplete and rounded bordered result cards replace the reference’s two-column model/product results and open rows. The shared full-screen panel uses a separate dark/light surface treatment and a generic logo top bar.
- **Recovery:** Preserve `SearchContext`, `buildSearchIndex`, `searchModels`, `searchProducts`, inquiry context, availability checks and 30-result “Show more” behavior. Reshape `SearchPanel` to show matching models in a distinct model column and products as reference-style rows; retain loading/error/empty/retry states and focus management. Adapt `ShopFullscreenPanel` presentation only as required; do not replace cart/auth logic. At mobile widths stack model results before products, keep input and clear action reachable, and avoid horizontal overflow.
- **Acceptance:** opening search focuses the input; Escape/backdrop/close restores focus; model selection routes to the current localized model URL; product selection opens its current slug URL; result rows show current price/availability and inquiry versus add-to-cart; loading, failure, no-results and clear states remain usable at 375px and desktop.

### S3R.2 — Side navigation rulers

- **Reference:** `src/partials/head.html` inserts both rails across reference pages. `assets/css/components.css` defines `.rail`, `.rail--left`, `.rail--right`, `.rail__ticks`, `.rail__sect`, `.rail__segment`, `.rail__marker` and `.rail__scale`. Rails are fixed from `top:var(--header-h)` to viewport bottom, one `var(--rail)` wide, with mono labels and hairline borders; they appear only at `min-width:1280px`. Left rail is section navigation: `assets/js/motion.js` builds ticks/section buttons from page sections and `data-section-label`, then uses ScrollTrigger to update `.is-cur`. Right rail is scroll progress: its marker follows document progress and clicking the ruler scrolls to the corresponding document position. They are functional editorial navigation, not decoration.
- **Shop:** `ShopShell.js` renders header, main content, footer and CTA dock, but no rail component/markup or rail controller exists in `app/`.
- **Recovery:** Add one public-shell rail component scoped to public Shop routes. Generate left section links from explicitly labelled, navigable page sections; do not turn every arbitrary DOM section into a destination. Keep the right ruler’s proportional document-scroll behavior. Use existing header height, mono typography, neutral hairlines and accent active state. Use native links/buttons or equivalent keyboard-accessible controls and account for sticky header offsets. Hide both rails below 1280px, as the reference does; this also avoids collision with the tablet/mobile CTA dock.
- **Acceptance:** only sections with useful navigation appear on the left; active section updates during scroll; click and keyboard activation navigate to the section; right marker tracks page progress and click seeks to the chosen progress; rails start below the header, do not cover content, and are absent below 1280px.

### S3R.3 — Product cards and grids

- **Reference card:** `src/pages/shop/iphone-14.html` uses `.pgrid` and `.pcard` (generated by `assets/js/shop.js` model/catalog rendering). `assets/css/shop.css` defines image-first cards with a square `.photo`, contained image with 8% padding, type label, name, price/availability divider, and vertically stacked actions. Hairline `gap:1px` and background create shared separators; surfaces are flat, with no rounded-card shadow. `assets/css/shop-polish.css` gives the light catalog a 4:5 white image area, restrained padding and three-column editorial grid. CTA and availability are rendered by the data-driven card template; production must retain the S1 price/stock rule.
- **Shop:** `ProductCard.js` and `app/page.module.css` serve homepage/catalog cards. S3C overrides make cards flat and use hairline separators, but card media still uses the prior overlay type-pill pattern and commerce presentation remains a different composition. The effective catalog `.grid` rules are the later overrides: base 4 columns; 3 columns at 768–1279px; 2 columns at `max-width:1024px` (overriding the 3-column range); and 1 column at `max-width:600px`. Thus observed desktop/tablet/mobile counts are 4 at 1440px, 2 at 1024px, and 1 at 375px. The reference `.pgrid` rules are 3 columns by default for the light design, 2 at `max-width:1023px`, and 2 columns at mobile; at 1440px/1024px/375px it is 3/2/2. The defining mismatch is the Shop-only `max-width:600px` single-column rule and four-column desktop default; the 1024px boundary also differs by one pixel in its inclusive/exclusive convention only if using a min-width conversion.
- **Recovery:** Update the effective grid rules in `app/page.module.css` to match 3 columns above 1023px and 2 at/below 1023px, including mobile. Remove obsolete conflicting breakpoint blocks rather than stacking another override. Refine `ProductCard` hierarchy to the reference anatomy: image, type, title/SKU, retail/authorized wholesale, distinct availability, then the single appropriate CTA; maintain consistent card alignment for long names and absent images. Keep valid-price/in-stock add-to-cart and missing-price/out-of-stock inquiry, with `resolveProductLine` and existing `PartInquiryCard`/`AddToCartButton` logic.
- **Acceptance:** computed columns are 3/2/2 at 1440/1024/375px; cards have the same flat separated grid rhythm, contained images, readable titles and bottom-aligned commerce; all price/stock states and keyboard focus remain clear; no invented content or changed business rules.

### S3R.4 — Product detail

- **Reference:** `src/pages/shop/product.html` section order is breadcrumb, one contained `.product` two-column area (gallery then details), then trust strip, then inquiry card. The details order is type, large title, SKU, price block (label, prominent price, availability), purchase controls, concise description, service repair link, compatibility list. `assets/css/shop.css` uses a 7:5 gallery/details split from 1024px, square contained gallery, minimal border treatment and large price; `assets/css/shop-polish.css` removes the main-gallery border, enlarges its minimum height (620px at desktop), and scales the price. Mobile stacks naturally and removes fixed gallery minimum height.
- **Shop:** `app/(shop)/product/[slug]/page.js` currently renders breadcrumb and `.productLayout`, with `ProductGallery`, `.details`, type, title, SKU, prices, availability, `ProductPurchase`, description, `ServiceLinkCard`, then compatibility. SEO metadata/JSON-LD and server-loaded catalog data are present. `CatalogPages.module.css` has legacy rounded bordered gallery, compact page/title and compatibility cards; subsequent S3C overrides flatten some surfaces and change the split to 7:5, but the original detail information order differs: service link precedes compatibility, and there are no reference-equivalent trust/inquiry supporting sections on this route.
- **Recovery:** Keep the production data and security boundary. Recompose styles/markup to the reference hierarchy, reusing `ProductGallery`, `ProductPurchase`, `ServiceLinkCard`, `Breadcrumbs` and compatibility data. Keep structured compatibility separate from product description. Style wholesale information only for authorized users as it is today. Add the established trust/inquiry support only if it can use existing public settings and inquiry workflow; do not copy static facts or fabricated values from the mockup.
- **Acceptance:** desktop 7:5 gallery/details composition, responsive single-column stack, reference information hierarchy and contained imagery; valid, missing-price and out-of-stock CTA states remain correct; compatibility links, service links, metadata/JSON-LD and authorized wholesale visibility remain intact.

### S3R.5 — Page transitions

- **Reference:** `assets/js/motion.js`, `transitions()` (around lines 366–384), `assets/css/components.css` `.scan`/`.shutter`, and `src/partials/head.html`/`scripts-shop.html`. For unmodified same-site links (excluding hashes, external/mail/tel, `_blank`, modifier-key clicks and `[data-no-transition]`), it closes open UI, moves a 1px cyan scan line down the viewport while a paper shutter covers the page, then assigns `window.location.href`. Both run about 0.45s with `power2.inOut`; persisted `pageshow` resets the cover. Browser back/forward is not intercepted by the click handler; native navigation/BFCache is retained. `motion.js` adds `.no-motion` if GSAP/ScrollTrigger are absent or `?motion=off`; `base.css` and the script disable reveals under reduced motion. Scroll reveal, hover, and drawer animations are separate systems.
- **Shop:** no route-level transition implementation was found. `ShopFullscreenPanel.module.css` animates panel entry and a drawer scan line, but that is an overlay transition, not page navigation. Shop uses Next App Router and `basePath: "/shop"`; direct `window.location` interception would bypass client navigation and risks losing shell/cart/auth state.
- **Recovery:** Implement a small client-side App Router transition layer that reproduces the scan/shutter cue around internal route changes using existing CSS/WAAPI or a small local controller; avoid adding GSAP solely for this. Integrate with Next links/router and basePath-safe internal URLs; exclude anchors, external links, downloads, modified clicks and panel actions. Do not delay navigation in a way that loses intent or mutates cart/auth state. On popstate, avoid blocking native back/forward; reset any persisted cover and respect scroll restoration. Disable or shorten nonessential motion for `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid costly full-page filters and test on mobile.
- **Acceptance:** only eligible same-app route navigation animates; links still route under `/shop`; search/cart/auth/inquiry actions do not trigger page cover; back/forward and scroll restoration work; reduced-motion users receive immediate/near-immediate navigation; no stuck shutter on cached return or route error.

## Grid and breakpoint comparison

| Viewport | Approved light reference `.pgrid` | Current Shop `.grid` effective cascade |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1440px | 3 columns | 4 columns |
| 1024px | 3 columns (`max-width:1023px` does not apply at exactly 1024) | 2 columns (`max-width:1024px`) |
| 375px | 2 columns | 1 column (`max-width:600px`) |

Reference CSS originates in `assets/css/shop.css` (base 2, 3 at 768px, 4 at 1280px) but is intentionally overridden for the light editorial theme by `assets/css/shop-polish.css`: 3 columns by default, 2 at `max-width:1023px`. Therefore the actual reference count at exactly 1024px is **3**; at 1023px and below it is **2**. Shop’s later `app/page.module.css` rules make the grid 4 by default, 3 at 768–1279px, 2 at 1024px and below, and 1 at 600px and below. Target counts: 1440px **3**, 1024px **3**, 375px **2**. Reference product-grid gap is 1px hairline separators; the reference card image is 4:5 in light theme. Shop uses 1px separator treatment in its later override, but does not match the reference column rule.
